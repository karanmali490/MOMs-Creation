Who We Are

Crafted With Purpose,
Built for Quality

Mom's Textile is a Pune-based manufacturer and supplier of premium custom textile products. We started with a simple belief: that everyday textile items — tote bags, home furnishings, accessories — could be both beautiful and durable.

From our origins introducing customised products to the market, we've grown into a full-range manufacturer covering everything from Sanchi tote bags and professional bags to baby products, pet accessories, diaries, and lady purses.

Every product in our catalog is made with carefully sourced fabrics — cotton, canvas, denim, jute, crochet — crafted to handle bulk production without compromising on finish or quality.

🎯
